# Demo data (synthetic)

`orders.csv`, `order_lines.csv` and `products.csv` are generated by
`reporting-pipeline generate-demo-data` (seed 20260701): 24 months of orders of a fictitious
office-equipment retailer (July 2024 – June 2026), with a growth trend, a year-end peak and a changing
category mix. All names and identifiers are invented; amounts are in EUR. No real person or company is
represented.

The files contain **15 planted anomalies** (invalid dates and numbers, duplicates, unknown references,
values to normalize) used to demonstrate the quality checks; they are listed in
[docs/data-quality.md](../../docs/data-quality.md#planted-anomalies-in-the-demo-data). Columns, types
and keys: [docs/data-model.md](../../docs/data-model.md).

Regenerating gives byte-identical files (tested):

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\reporting-pipeline.exe generate-demo-data --output data/sample --overwrite
```
